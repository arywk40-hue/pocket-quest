# A 90-second demo, filmed after real use

0–12s: Show the case screen and explain: “Outside Case turns a familiar walk into a short audio mystery. The story is fictional; the observations are real.”

12–25s: Download the field kit and open it on the actual phone. Show the transcript or tested device speech, and name the phone and browser.

25–45s: Film a participant listening to one chapter, pocketing the phone, and looking at a fallen leaf. Avoid showing identifiable bystanders without permission.

45–65s: Import the exported journal into the local companion. Show an actual Gemma photo review and its resulting fictional continuation. Include an unrelated or ambiguous photo to demonstrate a refusal/uncertainty, rather than only the happiest path.

65–78s: If a real Sentry trace has arrived in the dashboard, show its agent/model spans with body content absent and report the measured local latency. Do not use mock spans or a connectivity diagnostic as partner evidence.

78–90s: Show the ending and real field-trial results. Close with what failed and what you will improve.

Keep the demo concrete. No fake outdoor footage, made-up user quotes, invented speed numbers, or staged model outputs represented as live inference.
